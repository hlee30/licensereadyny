What the New York requirement actually is

New York requires 77 hours of approved qualifying education, a school-administered proctored final exam, and the separate New York State salesperson exam. The school final and state exam are different exams.

The Department of State's approved-school directory includes many more providers than the schools on this page. This comparison is limited to the providers I specifically researched for price and retake value.

See the full NYDOS approved school directory →

Important pricing notes

  • Sale prices and promo codes can change at any time.
  • Some providers charge separately for the school-final proctor.
  • The New York State salesperson exam fee is separate from the school's course-final exam.
  • My 360training base price comes from the Course Retake comparison; the provider currently confirms a separate $54 proctoring charge.
  • Where a provider's current public page did not clearly state a separate proctor fee, the table says to verify it rather than assuming it is included.

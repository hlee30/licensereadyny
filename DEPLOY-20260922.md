# License Ready NY analytics update — September 22, 2026

## What changed

### Lead conversions are now separated
- `daily_practice_signup`: fires only after MailerLite confirms the daily-practice signup succeeded.
- `real_estate_lead`: fires only on the Work With Me thank-you flow after a successful real-estate inquiry.
- The website no longer sends new `generate_lead` events, so the two lead types are no longer mixed together.

### Diagnostic form events
- `daily_practice_form_start`
- `daily_practice_form_submit`
- `real_estate_lead_form_start`
- `real_estate_lead_form_submit`

These are diagnostics, not successful conversions.

### Practice funnel events
Existing events are preserved:
- `quiz_ready`
- `quiz_view`
- `quiz_start`
- `quiz_answer`
- `quiz_practice_10`
- `practice_topic_click`

Added after the GA4 review:
- `practice_tests_view`
- `practice_tests_click`
- `daily_practice_cta_click`

### Real-estate services funnel
- `work_with_me_view`
- `work_with_me_click`
- `real_estate_lead_form_start`
- `real_estate_lead_form_submit`
- `real_estate_lead`

`real_estate_lead` also records non-PII `lead_type` (Buying/Selling/Renting/General question) and the selected timeline when available.

### Attribution preservation
UTM parameters and Google click IDs are stored for the current browser session and populated into the Work With Me form, so ad/source information is not lost when a visitor navigates from the landing page to the lead form.

Tracked fields:
- `utm_source`
- `utm_medium`
- `utm_campaign`
- `utm_content`
- `utm_term`
- `gclid`
- `gbraid`
- `wbraid`
- `landing_path`

No email address, name, phone number, form message, or quiz question text is sent to GA4 by `analytics.js`.

### Payhip funnel
- `practice_pack_click` remains.
- `begin_checkout` is now also sent for the $14.99 Payhip product click.
- No `purchase` event is generated from a click. A verified Payhip purchase integration is still required before using `purchase` as a conversion.

CONTRACTS EXAM PREP

What Should You Know About Contracts for the NY Real Estate Exam?

Contract questions can test whether an agreement is enforceable, what creates a valid contract and how an existing agreement can be changed or ended. Important concepts include consideration, mutual assent, contractual capacity, the Statute of Frauds, amendments, counteroffers, assignments, novation and rescission.

This practice set focuses specifically on real estate contract concepts so you can review them separately before returning to the mixed New York real estate exam practice test.

STATUTE OF FRAUDS

Practice Statute of Frauds Questions

Real estate exam questions may ask which agreements generally must be in writing to be enforceable. Understanding how the Statute of Frauds applies to real property transactions is an important part of contract preparation.

CONTRACT FORMATION

Review Consideration, Capacity and Mutual Assent

A valid contract depends on more than simply agreeing on a price. Practice questions may test consideration, contractual capacity, agreement to material terms and whether an offer has actually been accepted.

Learning to distinguish an acceptance from a counteroffer can help you answer transaction-based questions more confidently.

CHANGING AN AGREEMENT

Amendment, Novation and Rescission

These terms can sound similar, but they describe different situations. An amendment changes an existing contract, while novation generally substitutes an obligation or party with agreement of the parties. Rescission involves undoing the agreement.

The practice questions on this page use transaction scenarios to help you distinguish among these concepts.

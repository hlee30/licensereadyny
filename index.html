PRACTICE TEST FAQ

NY Real Estate Exam Practice Test FAQ

Are the NY real estate practice questions free?

Yes. You can use the mixed quiz and topic-based practice questions on License Ready NY for free.

What topics can I practice for the New York real estate exam?

The question bank includes agency, contracts, finance, valuation, fair housing, property ownership, real estate practice, commercial real estate, construction and additional real estate concepts.

Is there a separate NYC real estate salesperson exam?

Real estate salesperson licensing is handled at the New York State level, so people planning to work in New York City prepare for the New York State salesperson licensing exam.

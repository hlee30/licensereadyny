What Topics Should You Study for the New York Real Estate Salesperson Exam?

Use the official New York 77-hour curriculum to organize your study plan, then practice the major subjects one at a time.

Reviewed September 22, 2026 · Based on current New York State Department of State guidance.

QUICK ANSWER

What should you study for the NY real estate salesperson exam?

Use the New York Department of State's 77-hour salesperson curriculum as your core study outline. It covers agency, legal issues, contracts, finance, land use, construction and environmental issues, valuation, fair housing, mathematics, insurance, condominiums and cooperatives, commercial and investment property, property management and additional licensing subjects.

The official curriculum is the safest study framework

New York publishes the required 77-hour salesperson curriculum with instructional hours assigned to each subject. That curriculum is a reliable way to make sure your study plan covers the full range of material rather than focusing only on the topics that appear most often in third-party practice questions.

The state curriculum does not, however, say that the instructional-hour percentages equal the exact number of questions you will receive on the state exam. Treat the hours as course structure, not as a guaranteed exam blueprint.

Which subjects receive the most course hours?

In the official curriculum, law of agency receives 11 hours; legal issues receive 10 hours; commercial and investment properties receive 10 hours; human rights and fair housing receive 6 hours; and both real estate finance and construction and environmental issues receive 5 hours.

Those hour totals can help you understand the depth of the coursework, but they are not a published promise about the exact distribution of state-exam questions.

Frequently asked questions

Does New York publish an exact number of exam questions for each topic?

The official curriculum publishes instructional hours by subject, but the course hours should not be treated as an official state-exam question distribution.

Which subjects have the largest curriculum blocks?

Agency, legal issues and commercial/investment property are among the largest, followed by fair housing, finance, and construction/environmental issues.

Should I only study the largest subjects?

No. The official curriculum covers 19 subject areas, so a complete study plan should include smaller topics as well as the larger course blocks.

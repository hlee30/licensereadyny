AGENCY EXAM PREP

What Should You Know About Agency for the NY Real Estate Exam?

Agency questions test your understanding of the relationship between a real estate licensee and the people represented in a transaction. Important concepts include fiduciary duties, confidentiality, disclosure, loyalty, obedience, accounting, reasonable care and the different types of agency relationships.

This practice set focuses specifically on agency concepts so you can review the topic separately before returning to a mixed New York real estate exam practice test.

FIDUCIARY DUTIES

Practice Fiduciary Duty Questions

Agency relationships can create duties such as loyalty, confidentiality, disclosure, obedience to lawful instructions, accounting and reasonable care.

Practice questions can help you distinguish between duties that sound similar and apply them to realistic real estate scenarios.

AGENCY RELATIONSHIPS

Review Special, General and Dual Agency

Agency questions may require you to identify the type of authority an agent has or determine which relationship is created by the facts of a transaction.

The practice bank includes questions involving special agency, general agency, dual agency and representation of buyers and sellers.

STUDY STRATEGY

How to Use This Agency Practice Test

Answer the questions one at a time and read the explanation after each response. If you miss a question, focus on why the correct answer applies rather than memorizing only the answer choice.

Once agency concepts feel comfortable, switch to the mixed quiz so you have to identify agency questions without being told the topic in advance.

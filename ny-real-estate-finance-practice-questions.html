FINANCE EXAM PREP

What Should You Know About Finance for the NY Real Estate Exam?

Finance questions may require you to calculate loan-to-value ratios, identify the meaning of mortgage points or recognize disclosure concepts associated with consumer credit.

Practice these concepts separately before returning to mixed exam practice.

LOAN-TO-VALUE

Practice LTV Calculations

Loan-to-value compares the amount borrowed with the relevant property value or purchase price used in the question. Read each problem carefully before calculating the percentage.

MORTGAGE POINTS

Review Mortgage Points

One mortgage point generally represents one percent of the loan amount. Finance questions can test your ability to distinguish loan-based calculations from calculations based on the property's purchase price.

CREDIT DISCLOSURES

APR and Regulation Z

Practice identifying APR and other consumer-credit terminology that may appear in real estate financing questions.
